# Co-production & Funding Annex

*Pierre et Mademoiselle* · prepared 12 August 2026
To accompany the series bible and the 50-page sample

**The short answer to "is there money for this":** yes, and this project is unusually well positioned for it — not because it can be engineered to qualify, but because it qualifies by nature.

---

## 1 · The instrument: the Brazil–France co-production agreement

✅ **Verified.** Brazil and France signed a bilateral audiovisual **co-production agreement in 2017**, between **ANCINE** (Agência Nacional do Cinema) and the **CNC** (Centre national du cinéma et de l'image animée).

Critically: a subsequent protocol between the two agencies **extended the agreement from cinematographic production alone to all audiovisual modalities** — which is what brings a limited series inside it. Ministry-level meetings between the Brazilian Secretaria do Audiovisual and the CNC have continued since.

**What a treaty co-production confers.** An official co-production is treated as a *national work in both countries*. That means it can access French support instruments as a French work **and** Brazilian support instruments as a Brazilian work, rather than being a foreign shoot in Brazil or a Brazilian show sold to France.

**The usual obstacle, and why this project doesn't have it.** Treaty co-productions require genuine creative and financial participation from both sides — and most projects have to be bent into that shape, which financiers can see. This one cannot be bent out of it:

- A **French protagonist**, in **French**, from Toulouse and La Rochelle and Versailles
- A **Brazilian setting**, in **Portuguese**, in Maranhão
- A city **founded by Frenchmen**, which the Portuguese kept along with its French name
- Tupinambá presence and language, and a mixed population that is the direct consequence of a French and a Dutch occupation
- Both national histories are load-bearing. Remove either and there is no story.

**Note for the French side:** the title of the enterprise Fermat sails under is *la France équinoxiale* — a real French colonial company, relaunched in **1663** and folded into Colbert's **Compagnie française des Indes occidentales** in **1664**. This is French national history that France itself has largely forgotten, recovered by a Brazilian writer. That is an argument, and it should be made in French, in the room.

## 2 · Brazilian instruments

✅ **Fundo Setorial do Audiovisual (FSA)** — created by **Lei nº 11.437 of 28 December 2006**, regulated by **Decreto nº 6.299 of 12 December 2007**. A programming category of the Fundo Nacional de Cultura, under the Ministério da Cultura. **ANCINE acts as executive secretary; BNDES and other accredited institutions act as financial agents.** The FSA is the principal public financing mechanism for Brazilian audiovisual production and has financed work at the top of the country's output — *O Agente Secreto* among it.

✅ **Lei Paulo Gustavo (LPG)**, **Lei Aldir Blanc (LAB)** and the **Política Nacional Aldir Blanc (PNAB)** — the post-2020 architecture of cultural financing, with which FSA policy is now being explicitly consolidated. ANCINE has recently run calls including a TV/VoD commercialisation edital of up to **R$ 60 million**.

⚠️ **Not verified and time-sensitive:** the specific FSA *editais* open for series in 2026–27, their categories, ceilings, and deadlines. **These change every cycle and must be checked directly with ANCINE before anything is submitted.** Do not put a number in a deck from this document.

⚠️ **Not yet checked:** Maranhão state and São Luís municipal incentives, and whether a Maranhão film commission exists. Given the subject, a state that would see its own founding story told globally is a natural partner, and this is worth an early call.

The CNC side of a treaty co-production is the counterpart to the above. ⚠️ **I have not verified the current terms of specific CNC programmes** — including *Aide aux cinémas du monde* and the series-side supports — nor whether any Council of Europe instrument such as Eurimages is reachable through the French partner. **A French producer will know this in an afternoon; do not guess at it in writing.**

## 4 · The location argument

✅ **The historic centre of São Luís is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, inscribed 4 December 1997** — the ninth Brazilian cultural property to be listed. It covers **220 hectares** and contains roughly **4,000 properties from the 18th and 19th centuries**, with the colonial urban fabric intact.

And UNESCO's own justification, near enough verbatim, tells this series' premise: an exceptional example of a Portuguese colonial city, with preserved layout — **founded by the French in 1612, invaded by the Dutch, and finally colonised by the Portuguese.**

**The set exists and is protected in perpetuity.** That is a production argument and a heritage argument at once, and it is the sort of thing that moves a state government.

Second location: **Marajó**, for the final movement — the largest fluvial island in the world, and the buffalo are already there.

## 5 · The heritage argument

Two elements of this story are recognised patrimony in their own right:

- **Bumba-meu-boi do Maranhão** — a bull that dies and is brought back. Registered by IPHAN in **2011**; inscribed by UNESCO as Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity on **11 December 2019**. The series never stages it — it is set fifty years before the form is documented — but the entire final act is about a lost boi, mourned and looked for, in the country where that song would later be sung.
- **The historic centre**, above.

This matters for public funding in a way it does not for a streamer. A ministry is not buying entertainment; it is buying the case that a national story is being told to the world with care. **The fact that the series deliberately declines to stage the *boi* out of period-accuracy — and says so in its own margins — is the strongest evidence available that the care is real.**

## 6 · What to send, and to whom

**The package as it stands:**

1. `pierre-et-mademoiselle-series-bible.html` — logline, premise, characters, episode architecture, tone
2. `reader.html` — 50 numbered pages of prose, printable, ending on *To be continued…*
3. `FACT_AND_FICTION.md` — the verification ledger; every date checkable
4. This annex

**Order of approach — the unglamorous one is the right one:**

1. **A French producer** with CNC track record and an existing Brazil relationship. Nothing else moves until this exists. A treaty co-production without a French partner is a wish.
2. **A Brazilian producer** with FSA experience. Same reason, other flag.
3. **Maranhão** — state culture secretariat and any film commission. Early, before anyone needs anything from them, purely on the subject.
4. **ANCINE / CNC** — to confirm the current mechanics of the extended agreement for a series.
5. **The streamers and publishers last**, with two national agencies and two producers already in the room.

⚠️ **On sending it to "publishers and Netflix, you name it":** don't. A wide simultaneous send is the one thing that devalues material that is otherwise strong, because the second reader always hears that the first passed. This is a French-Brazilian co-production with a documented historical spine and a UNESCO city attached — it should go to a small number of specific people, in a deliberate order, in the language of the country you are asking.

## 7 · One honest caution

Everything in §1, §2 and §4 above is verified and citable. **Everything about amounts, ceilings, current calls and eligibility is not**, and it changes annually in both countries.

The purpose of this annex is to establish that **the route exists and the project fits it** — nothing more. The moment anyone asks "how much," the answer is: *that is a question for our French and Brazilian producers, and we will have it in writing.*
