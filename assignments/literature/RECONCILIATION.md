## What survives

Everything in my draft is *post-arrival* material that I mistakenly staged during the arrival. None of it is lost — it is all in the wrong chapter:

- **São Luís as a French ghost** — a Portuguese city keeping the name of a French king fifty-one years after the French were thrown out
- **The commission he cannot spend** — real papers, correct seal, made out to a dead man; usable once, and once makes a pattern
- **The Le Mer children** — the blue eyes, the two occupations, the elders his own age and the young ones his grandchildren
- **They all behave like children** — never inducted into adulthood by any system that keeps records
- **What they know and who taught them** — Tupinambá competence through mothers and grandmothers; Europe left a language and an eye colour
- **The two ships that sailed away** — not sunk. They counted heads and made a sum.
- **The herd, dispersed and uncountable** — walking inland into exactly the wet country he bought them for
- **She keeps coming back** — the one that stays, at the same hour, for no reason he can write down
- **The cattle nobody counts** — *"wealthy in a thing they cannot count"*
- **o Conselheiro** — the title Brazil gives him, which is the job he crossed an ocean to escape

## The actual gap

**Nothing exists after `The Ceiling`.** That is where the story stops and where the next chapter belongs. The fort has taken them in, Marie is unconscious, and Fermat has just told the first useful lie of his new life.

The chapter that goes there is the one where he has to *stay* — and it inherits all the material above.

Working title, in your register: **What the Name Was Still Doing There.**

I have rewritten `what_the_shore_was_called.md` to sit in that slot instead of on top of yours. One thing I deliberately kept: he comes ashore holding nothing, which rhymes against `The Boy Who Carried Nothing`. She carried nothing out of Versailles by design. He carries nothing out of the sea by failure. Same condition, opposite routes — and only one of them chose it.
